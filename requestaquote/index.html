Pricing, VAT and calculation details

How the estimate is calculated

  • The calculator starts from the selected duration or package and then adds extra retouched images, additional photographers and optional production services.
  • Displayed amounts are gross orientation prices in EUR and include 20% Austrian VAT until company details, VAT ID and applicable reverse-charge conditions have been verified in the final written offer.
  • Travel, unusual location costs, extensive usage rights, complex production, special deadlines and custom art direction are confirmed in the final written offer.

Quick decision guide
  • Duration: choose a short headshot for one focused portrait; choose a longer guided session when you need more setups, outfits or communication uses.
  • Retouched images: select the number of fully finished images you want delivered. Extra images update the estimate immediately.
  • Teams / events: enter the number of people and available time; the calculator automatically recommends the minimum photographer coverage when needed.
  • Location: Vienna or Budapest studio is the simplest option; office or external locations add the relevant travel requirement to the estimate.

Where you see an i icon, open it for a short explanation of when that option is the right choice.

For quality portraits, one photographer photographs a maximum of 6 people per hour (about 10 minutes per person). If more people must be photographed in the same time window, parallel photographers and mobile studio stations may be required. Natural environmental portraits do not require a studio setup.
