Norbert Banhalmi-led photography team · Corporate · Leadership · Institutional events

The real story is not always happening on stage.

A handshake in the corridor. A glance from the front row. The laugh just after the official photograph. These small moments often tell the real story of an event.

We cover the important moments, of course. But we also watch the people, their connections and the feeling in the room. The photographs need to work in tomorrow’s press release and still mean something years from now.

How the work is built
BANHALMI

One team. One story.

I lead and coordinate the photography team personally. We work quietly with organizers, communications teams, protocol and security, dividing responsibilities so parallel moments, key participants and the atmosphere of the full event are covered without disruption.

I remain responsible for the visual direction, team structure, consistent image language and final selection. The resulting image set serves immediate communication and long-term institutional memory: people, space, scale, detail and the emotional rhythm of the occasion.

Process

How the event is documented

01

Preparation

We align on schedule, key participants, protocol, restricted moments, brand requirements and delivery priorities. I then define the team size, roles and coverage plan.

02

Coverage

My team and I work from coordinated positions, combining formal milestones with unscripted interactions and environmental context while avoiding unnecessary duplication.

03

Editing

I edit the full team output as one body of work. The final sequence keeps colour, exposure, emphasis and narrative rhythm consistent, regardless of which photographer made a particular frame.

04

Delivery

Priority images can be prepared rapidly; the full edited archive follows in the agreed format.

Project frameworkThe practical things worth agreeing before we start+

What does the project fee cover?

Before I photograph anything, we put the scope in writing: preparation, the shoot, selection, retouching, the final fee and any real travel or production cost. Work on location in Vienna and Budapest is included; anywhere else is agreed beforehand.

What arrives, and when?

You should not have to guess what happens after the shoot. The written offer sets the preview, selection and final-delivery dates, together with the file formats you actually need.

What happens to private material and image rights?

One person on your side can collect approvals so feedback stays simple. Unreleased photographs stay confidential. Your usage follows the agreed licence, and I do not turn client work into a public reference without permission.

What if the date changes?

Once the offer is accepted and any agreed booking payment is made, the date is yours. If plans move, the accepted offer already tells us how rescheduling, cancellation and documented outside costs are handled.
