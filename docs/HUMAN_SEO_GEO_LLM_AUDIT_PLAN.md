# BANHALMI professional site — human voice, SEO, GEO, schema and LLM audit plan

## 1. Site role

`norbertbanhalmi.com` is the canonical professional website for current services, pricing, enquiries and the canonical Person profile at `https://www.norbertbanhalmi.com/about/`. The artistic archive is `https://www.banhalmi.art/`; essays and migrated blog posts live at `https://blog.banhalmi.art/`.

## 2. Voice system

### A. Norbert's first-person voice
Use for the homepage, oeuvre, method and service introductions.

It should sound:
- like an experienced photographer speaking to one person;
- concrete about the room, the photograph, the people and the intended use;
- calm and confident without inflated claims;
- interested in trust and attention rather than “perfect poses”;
- varied in rhythm, with occasional short sentences.

Avoid:
- abstract strings of business nouns;
- generic phrases such as “more than”, “not just”, “timeless”, “authentic”, “unique”, “elevate”, “seamless”;
- repeatedly promising “premium”, “strategic” or “visual trust” without showing what that means in practice;
- speaking about Norbert in third person inside first-person pages.

### B. Company and service voice
Use direct, plain language. Explain what happens, who is involved, what is included and where the images will be used. Keep legal, price and process language precise.

### C. Evidence and machine-readable voice
Schema, pricing data, memberships and source registries remain neutral. They must not copy the emotional tone of visible marketing text.

## 3. Copy audit sequence

1. Read the visible text without schema, navigation and legal boilerplate.
2. Identify generic phrases, repeated sentence openings and long noun chains.
3. Check that each page answers a real visitor question.
4. Replace abstract claims with a concrete action, situation or result.
5. Check first-person consistency on Norbert's pages.
6. Compare EN, HU and DE-AT for equivalent intent and natural local phrasing.
7. Verify that biography and metadata agree with the canonical life journey.

## 4. Required biography correction

The digital beginning is the MOL Y2K project, where Norbert participated as an IT specialist and began documenting the project on his own initiative with a basic 1.3-megapixel digital camera. Military service followed later. No page may state that the first camera entered his life during military service.

## 5. SEO audit

- unique canonical, title and description for every indexable page;
- complete hreflang clusters;
- one clear H1 and logical heading order;
- service terms used naturally rather than repeated mechanically;
- internal links use the correct language path;
- booking, contact, archive and blog destinations are unambiguous;
- no alias domain appears as canonical or hreflang;
- redirects terminate in one hop.

## 6. Schema audit

- canonical Person: `https://www.norbertbanhalmi.com/about/`;
- Organization: Banhalmi Norbert e.U. / BANHALMI;
- Brand, WebSite, WebPage, Service, Offer / pricing context, LocalBusiness or ProfessionalService where justified;
- Vienna and Budapest Place nodes use stable IDs and consistent addresses;
- Viko Speier remains a supporting contact, not a co-primary entity;
- memberships, clients, founded initiatives and ambassador roles stay separate;
- visible copy, structured data and machine-readable JSON must not contradict one another.

## 7. GEO audit

- Vienna headquarters and Budapest operating base are consistently represented;
- phone, address, map and WhatsApp destinations match their location;
- location terms support visitors without keyword stuffing;
- the professional site, art archive and blog are explicitly linked as one ecosystem with different roles.

## 8. LLM audit

`llms.txt`, `ai.txt`, entity files and service/pricing JSON must let an AI system distinguish:
- Person, Organization and Brand;
- professional services, artistic archive and blog;
- four principal service areas versus supporting capabilities;
- historical clients, current memberships, founded organisations and collaborations;
- verified facts versus interpretation;
- the MOL Y2K beginning versus later military documentation.

## 9. Release gate

The automated audit must fail when:
- the military-service origin statement returns;
- the canonical Person changes;
- the art archive or blog is described as the booking site;
- a visible key page exceeds the agreed generic-marketing phrase threshold;
- schema and visible biography disagree;
- an alias domain becomes canonical or hreflang.
