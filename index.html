A portrait is not a picture. It is positioning.

Before a meeting, a profile visit or a press appearance, your image has already said something.

BANHALMI builds that first impression deliberately — for executives, founders, experts, artists, actors and creative professionals, as well as the teams and organisations around them. The work can begin with one executive portrait or headshot and grow into a coherent visual presence across personal branding, brand communication, press, campaigns and C-level events.

The principle

Make the person clear before making the image impressive.

The work starts with one question: what must this image make clear about you?

Light, direction and setting follow that answer. The result should feel precise, credible and recognisably yours.
