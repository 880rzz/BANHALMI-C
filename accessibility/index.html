Accessibility Statement

BANHALMI aims to provide an accessible experience across desktop, tablet and mobile devices.

Measures implemented

Semantic headings and landmarks, a skip link, keyboard-operable navigation and galleries, visible focus states, descriptive alternative text, original image proportions, responsive layouts, labelled form controls, clear error states and reduced-motion support are included.

Target standard

The site is designed with WCAG 2.2 level AA as the practical target. This statement is a technical commitment and does not replace an individual legal assessment of the Austrian Accessibility Act (BaFG) or other applicable rules.

Known limitations

Third-party review content from Trustindex or Elfsight is loaded only after optional consent and can have accessibility limitations outside the direct control of BANHALMI. External archive pages and social platforms follow their own accessibility standards. The large photographic archive may contain artistic captions rather than exhaustive visual descriptions.

Report a barrier

Please email hello@norbertbanhalmi.com with the URL, device, browser and a short description of the barrier. Reasonable efforts will be made to provide the requested information in an accessible alternative and to correct confirmed issues.
