Fine Art · Intimate portrait · Artistic nude

Fine Art Photography. Images with a story to tell.

Fine art photography begins with a person and a story worth holding on to. Portraits and artistic nudes are created through trust, clear boundaries and sensitive direction.

For years, my personal work has explored identity, dignity, the body and memory. I bring that experience to every commission. It helps me make images that feel personal and true, not merely polished.

BANHALMI

We talk first. The camera comes later.

The session begins by setting the boundaries together: what may be shown, what stays private and what the photographs should carry. Nothing has to be performed, and the pace remains yours.

My artistic practice has approached the body through projects about natural beauty, cancer recovery, womanhood, fatherhood, sacred touch and the relationship between vulnerability and dignity. That history informs the care of each private commission.

Process

How the space is held

01

Conversation

We discuss the personal meaning of the project, boundaries, privacy and the visual direction.

02

Environment

The studio or location is prepared so that light, temperature, pace and the number of people present support safety and concentration.

03

Image-making

Direction is clear but never forceful. The body is treated as part of a human story, not as an object.

04

Privacy and selection

Images are selected together. Publication never follows automatically; usage remains governed by the agreed consent.

Artists & Performers

Choose by what you need — not by photography jargon.

Whether you are an actor, dancer, performer, model or creative professional, these three options are designed around how much variety you need from one session.

1-Hour Artist Session — €690

For a focused set of strong professional images. A good choice for actor headshots, casting portraits, dancer or performer profile images, or one clearly defined look or character.

  • Actor headshots or casting portraits
  • Portraits plus a small selection of movement images
  • One strong character or visual direction
  • Updated professional profile or portfolio images

2-Hour Artist Portfolio — €990

For a more varied professional portfolio. Two hours give us room for portraits, full-body images, movement, different characters, outfits or visual styles. For many actors, dancers, performers and models, this is the most balanced option.

  • Acting portfolio for casting
  • Portraits and movement images for dancers
  • Several outfits or character variations
  • Images for website, agency profile and social media

3-Hour Complete Artist Portfolio — €1,290

For a complete body of images for several professional purposes. We can build a coherent visual portfolio with multiple outfits, characters, portraits, full-body images and movement.

  • Complete actor or performer portfolio
  • Several characters or visual directions
  • Static and dynamic dance images
  • Material for casting, website, social media, press and agencies
