### EN FAQ (19)
1. Q: Who is a leadership portrait for?
   A: For founders, leaders, consultants, experts and anyone a client is likely to meet in a photograph before meeting in person.
2. Q: What is the difference between a headshot and a leadership portrait?
   A: A headshot shows clearly who you are. A leadership portrait says more about your personality, your role and the way you want to meet the world.
3. Q: How should I prepare for the session?
   A: You receive practical guidance on wardrobe, grooming, image goals and intended usage before the session.
4. Q: Can you help with posing?
   A: Yes. Direction is calm and precise, with attention to posture, expression, hands and the way the image will be used.
5. Q: Do you work on location?
   A: Yes. Sessions can take place in the Vienna or Budapest studio, at your office, at an event venue or at another agreed location.
6. Q: Can you provide styling, hair and make-up?
   A: Yes. A stylist, hair professional and make-up artist can be arranged when the project benefits from them.
7. Q: Can several people be photographed in one production?
   A: Yes. Executive teams, boards, founders and departments can be photographed with a consistent visual system.
8. Q: Can you provide more than one photographer?
   A: Yes. Larger productions and C-level events can be covered by a coordinated multi-photographer team.
9. Q: Is on-site immediate retouching available?
   A: Yes. For selected executive, event and press productions, images can be selected, professionally retouched and delivered on location during the assignment.
10. Q: Can you deliver print-ready files?
   A: Yes. On request, final images are delivered in print-ready resolution and colour profile, alongside web-optimised versions for digital use.
11. Q: How are the final images selected?
   A: Selection can be guided together during or after the session, depending on the production format.
12. Q: How quickly are images delivered?
   A: Delivery timing is defined in the written offer. Accelerated and same-day workflows can be arranged for selected projects.
13. Q: What does visual brand positioning include?
   A: It aligns image language, portrait style, visual hierarchy and usage across website, LinkedIn, press, campaigns and institutional communication.
14. Q: Do you photograph C-level events?
   A: Yes. Executive meetings, conferences, leadership events, award ceremonies and high-level institutional occasions are documented discreetly.
15. Q: Is fine-art or intimate photography possible?
   A: Yes, only within a respectful artistic framework, with clear consent, privacy and boundaries agreed in advance.
16. Q: Is the calculated price final?
   A: No. It is a non-binding preliminary estimate. The final written offer depends on scope, location, timing, usage rights, production complexity and tax status.
17. Q: How are usage rights handled?
   A: Usage rights depend on the project, media, duration and territory and are confirmed in the written offer.
18. Q: Do you support EU reverse charge?
   A: Yes. Eligible EU businesses outside Austria may receive a reverse-charge estimate after business and VAT details are provided and verified.
19. Q: What happens to my images under GDPR?
   A: Images and personal data are handled confidentially and only for the agreed purpose. Publication takes place only with permission.

   A: Je nach Produktionsformat kann die Auswahl gemeinsam vor Ort oder nach dem Shooting erfolgen.
